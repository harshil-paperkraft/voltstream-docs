`eu-west` is out of preview. Event payloads and delivery history created in an
EU environment stay in the EU — storage, processing and backups.

<!-- truncate -->

Pick the region when you create the environment:

```bash
voltstream environments create --name production --region eu-west
```

`us-east`, `eu-west` and `ap-south` are now all generally available.

## The region cannot be changed later

This is the part to get right the first time. There is no migration path
between regions, by design — moving data across a residency boundary is exactly
what the boundary exists to prevent.

If you need to move, create a new environment in the target region and re-point
your publishers. Historical events do not come with you.

## What this does not cover

Region controls where *we* store data. It says nothing about where your
subscribers' servers are — an EU environment will happily deliver to an endpoint
in us-east, because that URL is your choice. If residency matters for delivery
too, that is a constraint on the endpoints you register.

A data processing addendum covering all three regions is available; email
support@voltstream.io.
